# T-116 — The printed contents page collides at thirteen entries, well below its measured bound

## 1. Specify

**Outcome**
The generated contents page lays out without collisions at every deck length up to its stated bound,
and the stated bound is a number that was measured against real entries.

**The mechanism, seen on paper**
Page 1 of the first adopting project's exported PDF — the contents page
[T-034](T-034-a-contents-page-for-the-printed-deck.md) generates, at **13 entries**:

- **the fourth row collides with the page footnote.** Card 13's last line and the footnote
  *"Detail behind the disclosure panels is on screen only — it is not on these pages"* print through
  each other;
- **rows two and three have touching card borders**, where entries whose bottom line runs to three
  lines overrun their grid track and meet the row beneath.

**Why the bound did not catch it.** `contents_bound.py` reports **16 as the bound and 24 as the hard
limit**, re-baselined by [T-084](T-084-the-contents-bound-fixture-counts-a-deck-that-no-longer-exists.md)
and unchanged since. Both numbers are *entry counts*, and an entry's height is not constant: the
fixture's cards carry shorter bottom lines than a real deck's. **A bound stated in entries, measured
on entries of one height, is not a bound.** The failure arrives at 13 against a limit of 24 because
the two are counting different things.

**This is a defect in the published plugin, and PH1 by the rule in [`../CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md).**
The contents page is generated by this repository, its collision reached a deliverable an adopter
presented, and no gate saw it: `printpages.py` asserts the page *count* — 14 for 13 slides, `n` + 1,
correct — and by the owner's ruling of 2026-08-08 it asserts nothing else. The count was right while
the page was broken, which is the exact shape **L-05** warns about.

**Scope**
- In: the grid track sizing, so a card sizes to its content and a row cannot overrun the next.
- In: the footnote's placement, so it cannot be reached by the last row.
- In: **re-measuring the bound against the tallest realistic entry**, not the fixture's. The new
  number is stated with the entry height it assumes, so the next person can tell when it stops
  being true.
- In: a fixture entry that is as tall as real content gets, so the bound is measured on the case that
  breaks it.
- Out: **the second sheet** — [T-036](T-036-the-second-contents-page-for-long-decks.md), which stays
  `PH2`. That task is a capability for decks past the bound; this is a defect below it. Fixing the
  track sizing may move the bound, and T-036 reads the new number rather than the old one.
- Out: DS-226's invariant itself. It is stated in du and is not what failed here.

**Inputs**
- [`tools/deck/contents_bound.py`](../tools/deck/contents_bound.py) — the instrument and its fixture.
- [`shell/components.css`](../shell/components.css) — the contents grid and the footnote.
- [T-034](T-034-a-contents-page-for-the-printed-deck.md) — what the page is and what it promised.
- [T-084](T-084-the-contents-bound-fixture-counts-a-deck-that-no-longer-exists.md) — the last
  re-baselining, and the argument for keeping the fixture hard-coded.
- Evidence: the adopting project's exported PDF, page 1. **Not copied into this repository.**

**Acceptance criteria**
- [ ] A 13-entry contents page prints with no overlapping cards and no text touching the footnote.
- [ ] The same at the re-measured bound, and one entry past it fails visibly rather than silently.
- [ ] Every entry has a three-line bottom line in the test fixture, since that is what broke it.
- [ ] The bound is restated with the entry height it assumes.
- [ ] `printpages.py` still reads `n` + 1; `contents_bound.py` green.
- [ ] **Looked at as a printed PDF**, not as a screen render — this defect is invisible on screen.
