# Meridian Infrastructure Fund — 2026 portfolio model

Illustrative. There is no Meridian Infrastructure Fund. Every figure below is an output of the
assumptions stated with it, and nothing here is drawn from a real fund, a real manager or a real
transaction. It exists so that a deck about portfolio concentration has figures that hold together
when a reader checks them against each other.

## The fund

Net asset value is **$2.40B** at 2026-06-30, across five sectors and 41 assets. The fund is nine
years into a twelve-year life. Net IRR since inception is **11.6%**.

## Allocation, five years

Share of NAV by sector, at each 30 June. Every column sums to 100.

| Sector | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Renewables | 31% | 36% | 42% | 47% | **52%** |
| Transmission | 22% | 21% | 20% | 19% | 18% |
| Digital infrastructure | 14% | 17% | 19% | 21% | 20% |
| Water | 18% | 15% | 12% | 9% | 7% |
| Transport | 15% | 11% | 7% | 4% | 3% |

Renewables rose **21 points** over the five years. No allocation decision moved it that far: the
sector simply revalued faster than the rest, and the fund did not sell into it.

## Where FY26's return came from

Total FY26 return is **12.4%**. Contribution by sector, in percentage points of that total:

| Sector | Contribution | Share of the return |
| :--- | ---: | ---: |
| Renewables | **+8.1 pp** | **65%** |
| Digital infrastructure | +3.4 pp | 27% |
| Transmission | +1.4 pp | 11% |
| Water | +0.2 pp | 2% |
| Transport | **−0.7 pp** | −6% |
| **Total** | **+12.4 pp** | 100% |

**Renewables is 52% of the fund and produced 65% of the year's return.** Those two figures are the
deck's argument and everything else supports or costs them. Renewables and digital together produced
**92%** of it.

A contribution is the sector's own return weighted by its average share of NAV across the year. The
two ends of the range:

| Sector | Sector return | Average share of NAV | Contribution |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Renewables | **16.2%** | **50.0%** | +8.1 pp |
| Transport | **−19.4%** | **3.5%** | −0.7 pp |

## Net IRR and volatility by sector

Since inception, annualised. Volatility is the standard deviation of quarterly valuation movements.

| Sector | Net IRR | Volatility |
| :--- | ---: | ---: |
| Digital infrastructure | 16.8% | 15.4% |
| Renewables | 14.2% | 12.1% |
| Transmission | 9.1% | 6.2% |
| Water | 7.4% | 4.8% |
| Transport | 5.9% | 9.6% |

Volatility is measured over the **36 quarters** since inception, on each sector's own valuation
series, excluding the two quarters around the 2023 valuation-policy change. A private valuation
series understates volatility against a traded one, and the ranking rather than the level is what
these figures are used for.

Transport is the only sector returning less than its volatility would justify, which is why it has
been allowed to run down to 3% rather than being rebalanced up.

## The two limits, and the breach

The investment policy carries two concentration limits:

| Limit | Policy | Actual | State |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | :--- |
| Single sector, share of NAV | 45% | **52%** | breached by 7 points |
| Top three assets, share of NAV | 30% | **34%** | breached by 4 points |

Both were inside policy at 2024-06-30. Neither breach was caused by a purchase.

The three largest assets, which are the second limit's subject:

| Asset | Share of NAV | Sector |
| :--- | ---: | :--- |
| Calder wind | **13%** | Renewables. Acquired 2019, revalued twice since. |
| Norbeck solar | **11%** | Renewables. Acquired 2021, the fund's largest single cheque. |
| Aldis transmission | **10%** | Transmission. The only one of the three outside renewables. |
| **Together** | **34%** | two of the three sit in the sector already over its own limit |

## What rebalancing to the 45% limit costs

Returning renewables to the 45% limit means moving **$170M** of NAV out of the sector.

| Line | Value | Basis |
| :--- | ---: | :--- |
| Value forgone on sale | **$7.7M** | $170M at a modelled 4.5% discount to carrying value |
| Redeployment drag | **$14.8M** | $170M at the fund's 11.6% net IRR, idle for a modelled 9 months |
| **Total modelled cost** | **$22.5M** | **0.9% of NAV** |

**$22.5M is what the recommendation costs**, and the deck states it before anyone asks for it.

### What moves the total

Both lines rest on one assumption each, and the total is only as good as the weaker of them:

| Assumption | Modelled | If it moves to | The line becomes |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Discount to carrying value | 4.5% | 7% | **$11.9M** |
| Redeployment idle | 9 months | 18 months | **$29.6M** |
| **Both together** | | | **$41.5M**, or **1.7%** of NAV |

If no buyer clears at a discount under **8%**, the programme returns to committee rather than
proceeding to tranche two.
