# Riverbend capital decision — programme timetable

Illustrative. Dates are the award window's, durations are the delivery estimates behind them.

## The window

The grant closes on 31 March 2027. The budget vote sits on 12 March, and it is the only meeting
before the close that can commit capital — nineteen days between the vote and the deadline.

## First benefit

| Proposal | First benefit | Why |
| :--- | ---: | :--- |
| Bike-share | **14 months** | siting and permits before the first station opens |
| Bus frequency | **4 months** | a schedule change, on buses the depot already runs |

Bike-share takes **14 months** to first benefit. Frequency takes **4 months**, because the change is
a timetable rather than a build.

## Weather

Bike-share is weather-limited on **94 days** a year, below 4°C or wet [est.]. Frequency has no
weather limit.

## The service change

Six routes at **12 minutes** peak headway is the modelled service. Nine buses cannot reach a
**12-minute** headway if the depot's cycle times run long, which is the first tripwire — the limit
is **12 min** and the modelled peak is **12 min**, leaving no margin.

## The review gate

The gate sits at month 18. Nothing changes for the public until month 18; then it is either **16
stations** on the ground or a service the city can withdraw.

| At the gate | Threshold | Modelled |
| :--- | ---: | ---: |
| Trunk ridership up | **18%** | **26%** |

Trunk ridership up **18%** is the threshold. The model predicts **26%**, so the gate clears with
room. If it does not clear, **$1.5M** returns to the reserve and the schedule reverts.

## The ask

On 12 March: **$4.1M** of the grant, **$6.8M** a year from the general fund, and **$1.5M** held for
the corridors where bike-share wins.
