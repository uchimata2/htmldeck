# Riverbend capital decision — ridership model

Illustrative. Catchments are counted from the residential register, not from ridership, so the two
columns below are comparable.

## Catchment

| Proposal | People reached | Boundary |
| :--- | ---: | :--- |
| Bike-share, 62 stations | **38,000** | within 300m of a station |
| Bus frequency, 6 trunk routes | **121,000** | within 400m of a trunk stop |

**38,000** people live within **300m** of a proposed station. **121,000** live within **400m** of a
trunk stop. The overlap between the two columns is **21,000** people, who are reached either way.

## The wait

Peak headway averages **22 minutes** across fourteen routes [est.]. Average wait is half the
headway, so **22 ÷ 2 = 11 minutes** — and the figure rises on any route where buses bunch.

One trip, door to door, takes **34 minutes** on the modelled corridor:

| Leg | Minutes |
| :--- | ---: |
| Walk | **6 min** |
| Wait | **11 min** |
| Ride | **13 min** |
| Transfer | **4 min** |

That is **15 minutes** not moving, or **44%** of the trip. Below roughly **10 minutes** of headway
riders stop timetabling their journeys and turn up instead, which is where the wait stops being a
planned cost and starts being invisible.

## Corridors

Corridor demand, weekday trips, each corridor being the catchment **400m** either side of one axis:

| Corridor | Weekday trips |
| :--- | ---: |
| Old Quarter | **4,200** |
| Riverside | **2,600** |
| Mill Row | **5,400** |
| North Line | **14,800** |
| Market Cross | **16,300** |
| Harbour Gate | **4,900** |

The three corridors bike-share wins carry **12,200** weekday trips; the six trunk routes carry
**36,000**. Bike-share wins **3 corridors**, about a quarter of trips, and it wins them on trip
length rather than on volume.

## Growth

Elasticity: each **10%** cut in wait adds **4%** ridership. Assumed for the whole modelled period
and not re-estimated year by year.

By 2031 the frequency package reaches **6,100** new daily trips and bike-share reaches **3,000**.
Bike-share flattens when **85%** of docks fill at peak, which the model reaches in year three. If
elasticity is half the assumed value, frequency reaches **4,000** by 2031 and still passes
bike-share.

## Transfers

Today a North Line rider bound for Market Cross changes at Centre from Route 3 to Route 7 and
stands **4 minutes**. At the modelled frequency the timed connection removes the change: **0
minutes** standing at Centre, on Route 1.

## The demand tripwire

Old Quarter demand passes **900 trips** a day if the corridor grows faster than modelled, at which
point the reserve buys stations early. Modelled demand there is **540** trips a day.
