What this asks of people

The reason code is the change

The most valuable change in the redesign is also the one most likely to fail.

The riskUsed once to make a point about a person, the log fills with the safest code.
The ruleFor the first year, codes are reported by code and never by manager.
The costThe question it answers needs a year of honest data before it can be asked.
Why it existsA forecast is a probability, not a guarantee. The reference framework names that limit, and the override is what human judgement does with it.

The model gives a probability, and a person still decides. For eight regional managers this redesign is less work and more exposure. Their judgement is private today. From now on the reason sits next to the number and can be scored next quarter.

The decision log is never used in an individual performance review.

The target is 95% of changes carrying a reason. An override is not a failure; an override nobody can explain later is.

Sign the safeguard before Phase 1 records its first reason code. Afterwards is too late.

Eight managers record why they changed a number, and the Board protects them for doing it.
