# L-109 — Making a note machine-readable buys binding first and satisfaction rarely; count which branch covers anything

Met 2026-08-16 (**T-165**). Every excusal in `check.py`'s `DEFERRED` already ended in prose with what
would close it — *CLOSES WHEN a second engine is in the harness*. The task turned that sentence into a
`(kind, subject)` field so a check could read it, hoping for the case nothing could see: an excusal
whose closer has landed. `staleExcusals` only fires when a rule is excused **and** checked, so
DS-004's reason half-died the day T-019 shipped DS-009's preflight and the account stayed a clean
partition for nine months.

**The field works, and the branch that was the point covers nothing.** Of six kinds, exactly one —
*another rule's check closes this* — is decidable on a run. Across all 27 live entries it holds
**zero**: amendment 10, look 7, work 7, deck 2, harness 1, **rule 0**. Nothing here is waiting on a
check somebody might write tomorrow; they are waiting on a rule's wording, a person's eye, a
measurement nobody has built, or a browser we do not have.

**What the field actually bought is binding, and that was the real defect.** A subject now has to
resolve — to a rule the ruleset owns, or to a phrase somebody can act on. All 27 bind today, and an
entry pointing at nothing fails the run from here on. That is a smaller claim than *the excusal
reports itself stale* and a much better one than a sentence nobody can evaluate.

**So: count the coverage of each branch before describing what the mechanism decides.** A design with
one enforceable arm and five declarative ones reads, in a summary, as *closing conditions are now
checked* — and it is nearer to *closing conditions now have to point at something*. Print the count
per branch including the zeroes, the way a rule with no verdict is printed SILENT rather than left
out (**L-36**): a kind that appears only when something is in it is one nobody can watch go empty.

**And do not manufacture the case you hoped for.** Two entries here could have been read as
*rule*-kind — their prose says another rule covers them in substance — and classifying them that way
would have turned the branch green and the gate red on a reading that is the owner's to make, not the
implementer's. The zero is the honest measurement.
