# L-116 — A document that recomputes its finding after fixing it reports the remedy, not the finding

A generated report is normally better than a written one: it cannot go stale, and re-running it
re-derives every figure. **That property inverts the moment the report is about a defect the same
piece of work then fixes.** Re-running it measures the tree after the repair, so the count comes out
zero and the document says nothing was ever wrong — with every number in it correct.

The case:
[T-119](../../tasks/T-119-audit-the-ruleset-for-rules-that-cost-more-than-they-return.md) found
thirteen rules that no instrument applied, and fixed them by naming them in `EVALUATION.md`'s
dimension lists and in the build pipeline. `docs/RULESET-AUDIT.md` is generated, and computed that
set live. Regenerating it after the fix printed **0 rules with no instrument** into a paragraph
whose subject was thirteen. The audit erased its own result, and the failure is silent: nothing is
inconsistent, no check can see it, and the sentence still parses.

**The fix is to split the two claims, because they are different claims.** The finding is *what was
found on a date* — it is history and it is pinned. The remedy is *what holds now* — it is measured
on every run and it must be measured, because that is what catches a later edit undoing the fix. The
document states both:

> Still uninstrumented on this run: **0** — none.

**How to apply.** When a generated document reports a defect its own work removes, ask what a
regeneration in six months should print. If the answer is *the same finding*, the finding is data
and gets pinned with its date. If the answer is *whether it came back*, that is a second figure and
it is the one to compute. Writing only the computed half loses the finding; writing only the pinned
half loses the regression. This is **L-08**'s derive-don't-store rule meeting its boundary: a
measurement of a fixed defect is not a derivable fact about today, it is a record of yesterday.
