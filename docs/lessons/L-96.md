# L-96 — A survey inherited from a closed task is evidence about the day it was taken

Found 2026-08-14 by [T-152](../../tasks/T-152-give-look-at-the-rendered-deck-one-operative-home.md),
which was raised from a remainder that
[T-144](../../tasks/T-144-give-each-cumulative-rule-one-operative-home.md) had surveyed and not taken.
The survey was one day old, written by a careful pass, and **wrong in both directions**.

It named [`EVALUATION.md`](../EVALUATION.md) as one of three live documents stating *look at the
rendered deck*. That document has never stated it: its only uses of the word are a cell describing
what the render gate does and a rubric row about progressive disclosure, which is the same word in
another sense. And it **missed a copy inside [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md)'s own *Working method* 3**
— in the file the rule was being cut out of — while a third sat in
[`TASK-WORKFLOW.md`](../../tasks/TASK-WORKFLOW.md) §2, pointing at `CLAUDE.md` and restating it in the
same sentence.

**Neither error is carelessness, and that is why it is a rule rather than a note.** A survey counts
*mentions of a phrase*, and a mention is not an operative statement — a description of a gate and a
governing rule read alike to a search. The direction of the error is not random either: it
over-counts documents that talk about the subject and under-counts the document being edited, because
the surveyor is reading that one for its own reasons.

**How to apply.**

1. **Re-measure before editing anything, and treat the inherited figures as a starting list rather
   than a result.** T-152 carried this as an acceptance criterion, which is the only reason it was
   caught: *each copy is re-measured and the act it constrains is named, before any edit.*
2. **Test each hit for what it constrains, not for whether the words are there.** A copy that
   governs an act is operative; one that describes a mechanism, cites a rule, or records history is
   not, and deleting it is a different mistake.
3. **Search the file you are editing as hard as the others.** The copy in the document under the
   knife is the one a survey most reliably misses.
4. **A remainder handed on from a closed task inherits that task's staleness**, and nothing carries
   the date forward with it. Where a per-item band means a finding closes while its work continues,
   each instance re-counts — the count is the work, not the preamble to it.
