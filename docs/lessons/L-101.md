# L-101 — A document written to be sent is wrong at home in exactly the ways nothing here can check

Met 2026-08-14 (**T-157**), handing three registers of observations to the projects they were about.
The documents had been assembled, reviewed, split one per owner and gated for weeks. Every pointer in
them resolved; the reference checker said so, over 2,601 pointers, zero broken. **They were still
broken as delivered, in two ways, and both were invisible from inside.**

**Links.** A repository-relative link resolves in the repository that holds it. Copy the text into an
issue on a different repository and every one of them is dead — not reported dead, because the tool
that reports dead pointers runs at home, where they are all alive. The fix is a rewrite on the way
out, and the place for it is the sending step, never the stored document: the committed copy is right
as it is, and editing it to suit the destination breaks it where it lives.

**Status.** The three documents opened with a banner reading *ready to send*. That sentence is
addressed to the sender. It is exactly true while the document waits and exactly wrong one second
after it arrives, and the reader who most needs the first line is the one for whom it is false. What
the recipient needs there is what the document *is* — a report, unranked, expecting no reply.

**The general shape: a document has a location, and half of what is true about it is true only there.**
Links, status, tense, "this repository", "our" and "your". None of it is caught by a checker, because
a checker is a thing that runs where the file is. The test that finds it is to read the document
**as the recipient**, in the destination, before it goes — and the cheapest form of that is to send it
and then look at what rendered, which is the closing checklist's rule 3 doing work nobody expected it
to do for a text file.

The same asymmetry from the other side is a row in this project's own upstream register: the sending
side cannot know what the receiving side gets, so the receiving side is the only one that can check.
